Critical Issues

Critical

Experience pages render only skeleton loaders

All ~20 accommodation detail pages (x3 languages = 60 URLs) serve empty skeleton UI in the initial HTML. Content is loaded entirely via client-side JavaScript. Googlebot may not execute JS fully, meaning property names, descriptions, prices, images, and availability are invisible to search engines.

Critical

Duplicate title tags & meta descriptions on all experience pages

Every experience page shares the identical title "Expérience de voyage — Okeyo Travel" and the same generic meta description. Google treats these as duplicate signals, severely limiting ranking potential for your most important product pages.

⚠

High Priority Issues

High

Chat page indexed with no crawlable content

The /chat page is set to "index, follow" but contains no server-rendered H1, text, or meaningful content. It should be noindexed to preserve crawl budget.

High

Missing alt text on key images

Logo (header & footer), hero background, AI pattern, testimonial photos, and Payzone badge all lack descriptive alt text. This impacts accessibility and image search visibility.

High

No FAQPage schema on support page

The support page has 10 well-structured FAQs but no FAQPage schema markup. This is a missed opportunity for rich results in Google Search.

High

Missing LodgingBusiness schema on experience pages

Accommodation pages lack LodgingBusiness, Product, and Offer structured data. This prevents rich results showing prices, ratings, and availability in search.

High

Category page titles in ALL CAPS with no context

"CHEFCHAOUEN — Okeyo Travel" lacks travel keywords and proper casing. Should be "Chefchaouen — Hébergements & Expériences | Okeyo Travel".

●

Medium Priority Issues

Medium

No "About Us" page

E-E-A-T signals require clear entity information. No team bios, company story, or credentials are visible anywhere on the site.

Medium

Thin content on category & explore pages

Category pages average ~350 words (just listing cards). Explore page has ~300 words. Both need introductory content, travel tips, and destination context.

Medium

Stock testimonial photos reduce trust

All 3 testimonials use Unsplash stock photos. This undermines authenticity. Use real photos or avatar initials instead.

Medium

Minimal internal cross-linking

No "related experiences", "nearby destinations", or contextual links between pages. This limits link equity distribution and user navigation paths.

Medium

Preorder page indexed with thin content

The waitlist page has ~250 words and no long-term SEO value. Should be noindexed.

Medium

Heavy JavaScript bundle

15+ JS chunks loaded. Experience pages rely entirely on client-side data fetching, impacting both performance and SEO.
